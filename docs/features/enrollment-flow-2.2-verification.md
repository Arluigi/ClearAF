Readability is reconfirmed when reopening restored identity photos.

Clinical approval still requires an approved identity, complete immutable package, consents, current NC evidence and payment. Rejected identity, confirmed age/location ineligibility and post-payment safety exits queue the existing durable full-invoice refund/cancellation path. The identity form has explicit age/location stop actions and native confirmation, without collecting unnecessary photos from a known ineligible patient. Correctable upload errors retain drafts.

ClearAF 2a — patient app
Direction 2a · Letterpress · patient app

Every screen in the app

Twelve screens covering everything the current iOS app does: auth and onboarding, the photo record with its four share states and comparison, clinician-assigned AM/PM routines with versioning and completion recording, the completion calendar, check-in forms, messaging with photo references, and profile with reminders and export. Ink is the accent; ochre appears only for unread and prescriptions.

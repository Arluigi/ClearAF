01 · Character

A record kept properly, not a wellness app

ClearAF holds a person's face over months and a clinician's decisions about it. It should feel like a well-kept paper record wired to a capable clinic: warm, precise, quiet, printed. The old language reached for reassurance — gradients, glow, a skin score, a streak. This one reaches for credibility: real dates, real versions, real photographs, generous room around them, and no second accent colour to hide behind.

1 · Ink is the accent

There is no brand hue. Near-black on warm paper carries every interactive and selected state; ochre appears only for unread and prescriptions.

2 · Paper, not cards

Hairlines and space make groups. A container earns a border only when it is a distinct task or an editable record.

3 · Print geometry

Radius 4 and under for content; 1.5px hard keylines on photographs. Squarer reads older and more deliberate than rounded.

4 · Glass is chrome only

Floating navigation, capture controls, sheets. Never a reading surface, never over a photograph you're judging, never on the web.

5 · The photograph is the largest thing

Never tint, crop or beautify clinical images. Neutral mats, true 4:5, uncropped original always reachable.

6 · Numbers get their own voice

Dates, versions, counts and percentages are mono with tabular figures. They read as data, not prose.

02 · Colour

Warm paper, near-black ink, one ochre

One accent, and it is the ink itself. Ochre is the single hue in the system and it is reserved: unread state, prescription chips, and “waiting on you” counts in the portal. It is never a success colour, never decoration, never a button fill. Status is never colour alone — it always carries a word.

04 · Space, shape, depth

Hairlines and a short radius scale

Spacing

4 · 6 · 10 · 14 · 18 · 22 · 28 · 44. iPhone gutter 22pt. Section gap 16–22. Row padding 11–14 vertical. Portal gutter 30–32px, rail 190–210px.

Radius

0 photo mats, chips, segmented, calendar cells, table rows · 4 buttons and step boxes · 26 sheets and glass bars · 999 avatars, toggles, radio marks, the capture button. Nothing between 4 and 26. Native controls keep system curvature.

Rules over borders

1px rule separates rows and sections; 2px ink rule marks a major break. A full border means “editable or bounded”. No nested bordered boxes.

Depth

Content: no shadow. Glass chrome: 0 2px 8px /.10 + 0 12px 30px /.12. Segmented thumb: 0 1px 2px /.14. No glow, no coloured shadow.

05 · Glass contract

Where Liquid Glass is allowed

Glass is the one piece of platform texture the brand borrows, so it stays meaningful: it marks things that float above the record and are always available. Everything else is opaque.

Yes
  • Floating tab bar with the fused capture button
  • Sheets and modals over content, including the post-capture review sheet
  • Scroll-edge effect under a large title
  • Keyboard accessory row
No
  • Any card, row or reading surface
  • Over a photograph being judged
  • The camera — capture is the native iOS picker, unstyled
  • Second glass layer over the system tab bar
  • Portal — the web gets no glass, ever
  • Segmented controls (native, tinted only)

Respect Reduce Transparency, Increase Contrast and Reduce Motion — the flatter result is correct, not a regression. Use standard materials; never simulate glass with a manual blurred rectangle. Any text over glass must be measured against the composited background, not the token behind it.

06 · Components

The families

Buttons
Disabled + reason required

Filled ink (one per screen, full-width on iOS), outlined, text-underline. Radius 4. Disabled always accompanied by a sentence saying why.

Height is platform-specific — verify computed height, not padding. iOS is a touch target: 44pt minimum, pinned with min-height:44 + centred content rather than derived from padding — padding-derived heights drift under the floor when the real font resolves line-height: normal or Dynamic Type scales. The portal is a mouse target and density is its premise: 32–36px for row, toolbar and inline controls (8–9px padding at 13px type), rising to 44px only for a primary full-width action such as Sign in. Never apply the 44pt floor to a portal table row.

08 · Voice

Plain, specific, unsentimental

You missed your routine 😔

11 of the last 14 days
Skin score 82 · +3 this week

Sentence case everywhere. No emoji. No exclamation marks. Never promise an outcome, never imply improvement from photo order, never grade adherence. Dates are written out for patients (“2 Sep”) and mono-stamped for clinicians (02 SEP · 07:04).

10 · Review checklist

Before a screen ships

□ Exactly one filled ink button
□ No hue anywhere except reserved ochre
□ No card a hairline could have done
□ Glass only on floating chrome
□ Every number in mono with tabular figures

□ 4.5:1 for all text under 24px, mono labels included
□ Measured against the composited background, incl. glass and sunk tones
□ Light and dark checked on device
□ Largest accessibility text size doesn't clip
□ Keyboard focus visible on web

□ All nine states designed
□ Buttons: iOS ≥44pt computed, portal 32–36px
□ No bottom spacer on a pushed view
□ No score, streak, grade or promised outcome
□ Photos untinted, uncropped original reachable
